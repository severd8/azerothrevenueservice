# ARS Forever

**See how much gold a player has made and the most they've ever held, for you and anyone you inspect.**

Azeroth Revenue Service (ARS) reads the gold statistics the game already keeps. For yourself it shows how much gold you've ever picked up, the most you've ever held, and what's in your bags. For other players it shows the figures the game lets you see when you inspect them. Each look is saved on your computer, so you can come back later and see how someone's numbers have changed.

ARS is an updated rebuild of Forever IRS for WoW: Forever, with a new look, a settings window and fixes.

## What it does

- **Gold at a glance:** total gold acquired, most gold ever owned, and your own current gold, with where it came from: looting, quests, vendors and the auction house.
- **The full picture:** spending, auction activity, quests, kills, dungeons, deaths and profession ranks.
- **Opens beside Inspect:** inspect a player and their ledger opens next to the Inspect window. You can turn this off.
- **History you can search:** the last 20 looks at every character, kept on your computer, sortable and searchable, with a plain text report you can copy.

## Getting started

1. Install with the CurseForge app, or unzip into `Interface/AddOns`.
2. Type **/ars** in game.

Coming from Forever IRS? Keep it turned on for one login and ARS brings your saved history over, then you can turn Forever IRS off.

See the **Changelog** on the Files tab for what's new, and the [full guide on GitHub](https://github.com/severd8/azerothrevenueservice#readme) for every command and setting.

## Good to know

- **Gold acquired isn't profit, and peak gold isn't what someone has now.** Gifts, gold from alts, trades and selling ports or summons aren't broken out, so a gap between the numbers is normal.
- The numbers come straight from the game and can be incomplete or out of date. They're **not proof of innocence or wrongdoing**.
- A missing number shows as "not reported" or "unavailable", never as 0.
- **Private:** nothing is sent anywhere, no chat messages, no background scanning. ARS only asks the game for a player's statistics when you click or inspect.
