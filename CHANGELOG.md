# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release of ARS Forever (Azeroth Revenue Service), an updated rebuild of Forever IRS for WoW: Forever.

- **Everything Forever IRS did:** total gold acquired, most gold ever owned and your own current gold; income, spending, auction, activity and profession statistics; opening beside the Inspect window; the last 20 captures of every character; a searchable, sortable list of saved characters; and a plain text report to copy.
- **Your Forever IRS history comes along.** Keep Forever IRS turned on for one login and ARS brings its saved characters in, then turn Forever IRS off. Nothing is lost or doubled.
- **A new look** shared with the other Forever addons, in a smaller window. The long explanations moved into hover help: hover any number for what it includes or why it's missing.
- **An options window** (`/ars options`): opening beside Inspect, the ARS button on the Inspect window, the ledger's size, the minimap button, the welcome screen, deleting saved history, and Restore defaults.
- **A minimap button:** left-click for your target's ledger (or yours), right-click for the options.
- **`/ars check`** lists every statistic the addon reads, with the game's own name for it.
- **Fixes from Forever IRS:**
  - The ledger opens beside the Inspect window even after you've dragged it somewhere else.
  - Inspecting a second player while the Inspect window is open now reads that player too.
  - The copy report shows every line (long reports were cut off).
  - A game update no longer turns the source line red on every capture; it's a note in the hover help.
  - No errors where the game hides who a player is (often in instances); it says so instead.
